核となる読み

195 BPMのExtreme Speed Metalという過剰な緊迫感を、内容は「二度寝して遅刻しかける朝」に全投入したコメディ曲。電車に間に合うことで一度解決したように見せ、最後に社員証忘れで落とす。

STYLE RAW

Style

Extreme Speed Metal, 195 BPM, ferocious and relentlessly aggressive, The very first sound is an unaccompanied piercing high-pitched male metal scream; no instruments before it, Immediately after the scream, the full band explodes into crushing high-speed riffing, No instrumental intro, Heavy galloping riffs, dense distorted guitars, rapid double-bass drums and powerful bass maintain nonstop momentum, Sharp, heavy, memorable riffs; never sweet, heroic, ornate or neoclassical, Extremely high-register male heavy metal vocals with a razor-sharp metallic tone, powerful chest-driven high notes, piercing sustained screams, dramatic octave leaps and commanding aggressive delivery; no soft or pop-style singing, Fast verses lead into a clearly defined intense chorus, After the bridge, one brief 8-bar raw shredding guitar solo, then immediately back to vocals, Final chorus at maximum intensity, then a very short spoken outro, No extended instrumental sections, Dense 1990s metal production
